# Save the Jungle 🌴

A two-player local multiplayer game inspired by Bomberman, developed in Unity with C#.

About the Game

Humans are coming to destroy your home and turn your jungle into concrete! Fight back, destroy their bricks, and defend your land.

Strategically place bombs to eliminate your opponent while avoiding explosions. The last player standing wins, or the player who destroys the most bricks when time runs out.

## 🕹️ Controls

### Player 1 — Hare
- **W, A, S, D:** Movement
- **Right Shift:** Drop bombs

### Player 2 — Black Grouse
- **Arrow Keys:** Movement
- **Left Shift:** Drop bombs

### General Controls
- **P:** Pause / Resume
- **ESC:** Quit

## 💥 Power-Ups

- **Blast Radius:** Increases bomb explosion range.
- **Extra Bomb:** Increases the number of bombs you can place at once.
